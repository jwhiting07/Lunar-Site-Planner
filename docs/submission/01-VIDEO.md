# Video talking points

**Target: 28 seconds total. Hard limit from the supplied form: 30 seconds.**

## Points to get across

- Problem: lunar terrain can block sunlight and the view of Earth, affecting power and direct-to-Earth communication opportunities.
- Solution: compare landing locations, dates and mission durations in one interactive tool.
- NASA connection: JPL SPICE geometry and LOLA terrain data drive the calculations.
- Demonstration: choose a location, analyze it, open the timeline, and compare an alternative.
- Value: make the tradeoffs easier for planners, educators and the public to explore.
- Boundary: an educational prototype; Earth visibility is geometric and power depends on assumed hardware.
